Style: a minimal hand-drawn whiteboard doodle on a PURE WHITE background
(#FFFFFF, flat, no paper texture, no vignette).
Ink: clean black marker lines of even thickness. The ONLY colour allowed is one
blue, #245CFF, used for at most one or two key elements that the frame is about;
everything else is black line art, optionally with white or very light grey
flat fills. No other colours at all.
Forbidden: gradients, shading, glow, shadows, 3D, photorealism, texture,
background scenery, confetti, dots, specks, sparkles, stars, decorations,
borders, frames, watermarks, signatures.
Text: NO text anywhere except the exact captions listed above. A caption is
short black uppercase hand lettering, spelled EXACTLY as given (it may be
Cyrillic), placed next to its own element, and appears once. Elements marked
"no caption" have no text near them.
Composition: elements are simple, bold and readable at small size, clearly
separated by generous white space; nothing touches or overlaps. Arrows are
thin black hand-drawn lines that stop short of the elements they connect.
Use the whole {aspect} canvas but keep a white margin around the edges.
Draw ONLY the listed elements.
